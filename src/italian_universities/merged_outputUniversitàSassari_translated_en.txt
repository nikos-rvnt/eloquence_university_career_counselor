Expert in verbal and non-verbal communication in multicultural contexts, with specific attention to intercultural aspects; Expert in processes of transformation and territorial governance with particular reference to local development Graduates of the Master's Degree Course in FOREIGN LANGUAGES AND LITERATURES FOR CULTURAL MEDIATION AND TERRITORIAL DEVELOPMENT are able to carry out their activities both as instructors in the linguistic field and as foreign language correspondents, with a high degree of competence. In fact, they can exploit their interdisciplinary training in order to use personally (or teach others) the linguistic structures they have acquired through the studies carried out in the Degree Course, applying these structures to institutional-business-cultural relations with foreign countries (they are, for example, able to write letters in a foreign language, prepare documents that use sector-specific languages, communicate by telephone in a foreign language, take notes in a foreign language, etc.) in the various multi-ethnic and multicultural contexts in which they may operate, both in Italy and abroad.
The expected employment opportunities also include carrying out consultancy activities for local authorities and companies (for example, cooperatives or companies in the tourism sector), in local authorities and government institutions for the design of complex cultural and tourist events at multiple levels (municipal, regional, national and international) and professional activities in public relations, image and related professions. Furthermore, graduates in this Degree Class will become experts in the promotion of sustainable tourism and local development; experts in regional and local development programs and plans, consultants in the design and participation, also as trainers, of events that fall within the field of conference tourism; editors of texts for advertising; editors of technical texts; experts in the programming of travel agencies; consultants in parks and nature reserves; researchers, graduate technicians and related professions.
Characteristics of the final exam.
The final exam is public and consists of the discussion of a written paper (thesis), prepared under the guidance of a supervisor and co-supervisor. The thesis may be written, in whole or in part, in the foreign language chosen by the student. In the latter case, a complete translation into Italian must also be provided.
Knowledge required for admission.
To be admitted to the Course, it is necessary to have a degree or a three-year university diploma, or another qualification obtained abroad and recognized as suitable.
Admission is subject to verification of personal preparation by passing an entrance test and possession of specific curricular requirements, listed below:
18 CFU in the SSD of the first curricular language;
18 CFU in the SSD of the second curricular language;
6 CFU in the SSD L-LIN/01;
6 CFU in the SSD INF/01;
6 CFU in the SSD M-GGR/01 or M-GGR/02.
The methods of verifying personal preparation and the required curricular requirements are detailed in the Course's Educational Regulations.

Characteristics of the final exam.
To be admitted to the final exam, students must have regularly attended the training activities and obtained the credits provided for in the regulations.
The final exam consists of the development of a technical-practical project, under the guidance of a supervisor. This project must synthesize as much as possible the basic and specific skills acquired during the three years of training. The result of this activity (which may or may not include a demonstrator) must be summarized in a report (written report) to accurately explain the activities carried out and the results obtained. The report must also reveal a correct use of sources and bibliography, systematic and argumentative skills, and clarity in the presentation. The project is discussed before a specially appointed commission. At the student's request, the final report can be prepared in English.
Knowledge required for access.
To be admitted to the degree course, it is necessary to have an upper secondary school diploma or another qualification obtained abroad and recognized as equivalent. It is also required to have, or acquire, adequate initial preparation. In particular, the following are required: knowledge of the Italian language, spoken and written, logical reasoning skills, knowledge and ability to use the main results of elementary mathematics and experimental sciences.
As the course is taught in Italian and English, the student must have an adequate level of knowledge of the English language. If not present, a level no lower than level B2 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages must be acquired and verified during the first year of study, through initiatives agreed with the University Language Center and/or with accredited external bodies; to this end, 3 CFU are recognized for the student without a passing grade.
The didactic regulations of the study course specify in detail the methods of assigning these CFUs. The didactic regulations also provide for the assignment of additional training obligations, to be fulfilled in the first year of the course, in the event that the verification of the knowledge required for access is not positive.

Foreign Qualification Program, texts and objectives Professional status conferred by the degree.
Doctoral students receive specific training for carrying out scientific and technological research activities in public and private research institutions. Through specific programs, in collaboration with industries and companies, doctoral students can acquire advanced professional skills, develop and apply research results, and initiate business and technology transfer activities.
The expected professional opportunities concern the career of a researcher in universities and public bodies or in private research structures, in the sectors of territorial planning, environmental monitoring of resources, and innovative technologies and processes related to the activities of the agri-food industry, where specific skills are required, as well as agrometeorological services and environmental agencies. The establishment of research spin-offs for technology transfer to companies. The scientific qualification of the professional activities of the degrees of access to school (agronomist and forester, veterinarian, engineer, architect, biologist, naturalist, chemist, etc.). They can practice as a freelancer with specialization in the field of studies and research or increase their entrepreneurial skills in the agricultural, agroforestry and zootechnical, agri-food and territorial governance sectors, scientific dissemination and technology transfer of innovation. Employment in primary production and food processing industries, as well as in consulting and services in the relevant sectors, should also be considered.
Specific training objectives.
The course aims to train highly qualified doctoral students capable of responding to the demand for research and development in the fields of:
i) agrometeorology and plant ecophysiology;
ii) productivity of agricultural crops;
iii) management and protection of agricultural and forestry production systems; conservation, protection and enhancement of natural resources;
iv) biotechnologies aimed at the production needs of regional, national and international scope;
v) breeding and reproduction techniques, nutrition and feeding, applied genetics and selection of livestock animals, also using molecular biology techniques, quality and safety of food products of animal origin.
vi) The study of the forms of soil degradation, understood as the loss of productivity or utility of the soil, actual or potential, due to natural or anthropogenic factors that affect production and food security, livelihoods, and the production and supply of other ecosystem goods and services.
vii) Factors and forces that lead to soil degradation and desertification.
During the three years, doctoral students acquire the scientific method and the skills necessary for the preparation of experimental projects, the conduct of experimental plans and laboratory activities, the mathematical-statistical processing of data, the evaluation of statistical-experimental inferences and the dissemination of research results.
